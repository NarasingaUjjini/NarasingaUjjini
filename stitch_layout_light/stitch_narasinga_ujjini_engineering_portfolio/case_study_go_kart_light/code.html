Chassis Architecture

Predator 212cc

Integrated horizontal shaft OHV gas engine. Governor bypassed and fitted with a high-flow intake and custom straight-pipe exhaust for increased top-end RPM.

MIG Assembly

Gas-shielded solid wire welding utilized for all structural joints. Careful heat management applied to minimize warpage across the longitudinal rails.
